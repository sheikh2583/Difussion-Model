# Storage retention record

## 24 September 2026 cleanup

Disk cleanup was performed after all diffusion trainers had exited. The active
pretrained-codec validation was not interrupted and none of its inputs or
outputs were touched.

Removed artifacts:

- 204 checkpoint archive ZIPs (`33,341,940,696` bytes). Each was a duplicate
  packaging copy of a raw checkpoint plus small metadata already retained
  elsewhere.
- Two generated dataset checkpoint bundles in `results/exports/`
  (`9,752,705,885` bytes).
- 181 superseded intermediate raw checkpoints (`29,543,340,335` bytes).

Total reclaimed from the project was `72,637,986,916` bytes (about 67.7 GiB).
Project usage fell from approximately 86 GiB to 14 GiB, and filesystem free
space rose from 9.1 GiB to 77 GiB.

Retention policy:

- keep the highest available raw checkpoint in every checkpoint series;
- additionally keep MF CelebA latent epochs 80 and 100 because epoch 80 is the
  canonical pre-divergence evaluation and epoch 100 is divergence evidence;
- keep Hutchinson-CV epoch 90 as its latest recovery point;
- preserve the entire original `results/mf_hutchinson_celeba_latent/`
  negative-evidence directory;
- preserve all metrics, plots, configs, environment records, event streams,
  and training transcripts;
- preserve datasets, latent caches, Reflow pairs, pretrained weights, accepted
  codec records, FID reference statistics, the virtual environment, and Git
  history.

Deleted checkpoint archives and intermediate weights are not recoverable from
the compact thesis context. They can only be regenerated by retraining or from
an external backup. Epoch-level loss curves and research measurements remain
available in the retained JSONL metrics and logs.
